## 1.2.0

- New instruments: **square piano** (Happy Bay), **kalimba** (Al'Nilem) and **shamisen** (Kicia Bay).
- Colours fixed: the instruments now show their real colours (the wood looked greyish before).
- Instruments for sale can no longer sink into tables or the floor.
- New prices: instruments are now more expensive. Instruments you already own are not affected.

## 1.1.2

- Flute: it was held the wrong way round. The notch (mouthpiece) is now at your mouth, both when playing and when carrying it. If you never changed the flute position settings, they update automatically. Thanks for the report on Discord!

## 1.1.1

- Safer loading: the instruments are now registered at the exact moment the game builds its item list, so saved instruments can no longer go missing when a save loads quickly or with mods like Save Cleaner. Thanks Keevi!

## 1.1.0

- New instrument: **hurdy-gurdy**, sold in the tavern of Aestra Abbey. 10 notes, a drone while you play and a turning crank.
- Lute: plucks now vary in strength (soft and hard plucks).
- Flute: fixed clicks/static when changing notes quickly.
- Nakers: they no longer sink slightly into their table.

## 1.0.0

- First release: lute, xiao flute and nakers.
